Privacy notice

PCCguessr is an unofficial student project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pensacola Christian College. Its name and this page do not represent the college.

What we store

  • Email address. Used to sign you in. It is stored only in the sign-in system (Supabase Auth). It is never shown on the leaderboard or to other players.
  • Display name. The name you choose. It is shown publicly.
  • Game history and scores. Each game you start, your guesses, distances, round scores, and total score.
  • Timestamps. When you signed up, when games started and finished, and when you guessed. These are used for daily game limits, weekly rankings, and cheating checks.

Why we store it

We store this information to let you sign in with a one-time code, play games, keep score, show rankings, limit the number of games per day, and detect suspicious play.

Who can see what

  • Other players can see only your display name and your scores on the leaderboards.
  • You can see your own display name, stats, and game results.
  • The project maintainer can see all stored data, including email addresses, and flagged games, to run and protect the service.
  • Your email address is not shown publicly.

We do not sell or share your data

We do not sell your information and do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it for anything other than running this game.

How long we keep it

Account and game data are kept until you ask for your account to be deleted. Voided or abandoned games may be kept until then as well, for administration and integrity checks.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and all associated data, email the maintainer at CONTACT_EMAIL from the address you signed up with. Deletion removes your profile, game history, and scores.

Google Maps and Street View

The game loads maps and Street View images from Google. When you play, your browser connects directly to Google to load them. Google may collect information about these requests under its own policies. See Google's privacy policy.

Questions

Questions about this notice can go to CONTACT_EMAIL.
